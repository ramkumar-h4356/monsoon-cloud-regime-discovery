# M6.5 — Integrated Scientific Interpretation

## 1. Purpose

M6.5 consolidates the established-monsoon cloud-regime analysis into an integrated scientific interpretation. This is a documentation milestone. It does not introduce a new clustering model or modify raw datasets and previous analysis outputs.

## 2. Evidence Integrated

- Feature preparation and complete-case selection.
- PCA and refined t-SNE analysis.
- K-means model selection and physical cluster profiles.
- Initialization and sampling stability.
- Spatial and temporal prevalence.
- Annual coverage matching and period comparisons.
- M6.1 rainfall spatial-mask audit.
- M6.2 sampling stability.
- M6.3 cross-representation agreement.
- M6.4 rainfall uncertainty.

## 3. Integrated Scientific Findings

### 3.1 Dimensionality Reduction

Four principal components retain approximately 94% of variance in both baseline and log-sensitivity representations.

t-SNE provides exploratory local-neighbourhood visualisation. The refined runs reached iteration 2,999 of 3,000 without demonstrated convergence. Its global distances must not be interpreted as physical distances.

### 3.2 Clustering and Physical Interpretation

K=2 is a leading candidate based on silhouette and Calinski-Harabasz scores. However, Davies-Bouldin does not select K=2 for both representations. K=2 is therefore not a uniquely established optimum.

The baseline K=2 solution separates observations with comparatively lower cloud coverage, optical depth and ice water path from observations with higher values.

Cluster labels are arbitrary. Physical profiles, rather than numeric labels, must be used when comparing representations.

### 3.3 Stability and Representation Sensitivity

The fixed-initialisation sampling experiment found higher mean pairwise ARI for the log-sensitivity representation across the tested 50%, 70% and 80% sampling fractions.

Baseline stability was notably lower at 70%. The recorded MiniBatchKMeans runs stopped after 1–3 iterations despite a maximum of 200 iterations. Early stopping may explain this behaviour; it does not independently prove failed convergence.

M6.3 cross-representation agreement was moderate and varied with sampling fraction.

**M6.3 provenance qualification:** M6.3 used the earlier `sampling_stability_controlled` predictions, not the later `sampling_stability_fixed_init` predictions. Its findings must be interpreted using its recorded input.

### 3.4 External Rainfall Association

Rainfall matching covered 110,314 cloud observations, approximately 37.993% of complete cases, and 357 of 960 grid cells.

K=2 within-cell rainfall contrasts remained directionally consistent across the tested spatial block sizes and support thresholds in M6.4.

These findings apply only to the matched rainfall subset. They are observational associations and do not establish causality.

## 4. Scientific Limitations

- Complete-case filtering excludes 55,245 raw observations.
- The study covers a selected 30-day established-monsoon window.
- Cluster assignments depend on representation and sampling.
- K=2 is not uniquely optimal under every internal metric.
- t-SNE convergence was not demonstrated.
- M6.2 has an early-stopping qualification.
- M6.3 uses an earlier controlled M6.2 input.
- Rainfall validation covers only 357 cells.
- Rainfall results are conditional observational associations.
- Bootstrap uncertainty does not correct for missing rainfall coverage.

## 5. Integrated Conclusion

The analysis identifies interpretable statistical groupings in the selected cloud-feature space during the established-monsoon window.

The results support further investigation of lower- and higher-cloud-property groupings. However, sensitivity to representation and sampling, incomplete rainfall coverage, and the exploratory nature of dimensionality reduction prevent treating the clusters as conclusively validated meteorological regimes.
